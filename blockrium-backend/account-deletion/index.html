Account deletion request

This page lets users of the Blockrium - Web3 Network app request deletion of their app account and associated user data. Blockrium Web3 Technologies reviews deletion requests and completes verified requests within 3 working days.

Email deletion request Visit Blockrium

How to request deletion

  • Email support@blockrium.com from your registered email address.
  • Include your Blockrium user ID, UID, or registered email so we can identify the correct account.
  • You can also open the app and go to Profile or Settings, then choose Delete Account.

Data deleted

  • Account profile details such as name, email, username, UID, phone number, and referral code.
  • App wallet reward records, mining reward records, daily reward and task activity, referral activity, and notification tokens linked to the account.
  • Marketing or campaign preferences linked to the app account.

Data retained when required

  • Records required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, tax, legal, or regulatory compliance may be retained only as long as necessary.
  • Server backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before normal backup rotation removes it.
  • Public blockchain transactions cannot be deleted from a blockchain network, but Blockrium deletes the app account data it controls.

Temporary account deactivation is not treated as account deletion. A verified deletion request removes the account data controlled by Blockrium Web3 Technologies.
