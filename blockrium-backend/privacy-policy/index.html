Blockrium - Web3 Network

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Blockrium Web3 Technologies collects, uses, shares, and protects data when you use the Blockrium - Web3 Network mobile app and related services.

Effective date: 3 June 2026

By using Blockrium, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and request account deletion at https://blockrium.com/delete.html.

Data we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, username, UID, phone number, profile details, and referral code.
  • Authentication and security data such as login tokens, device identifiers, notification tokens, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • App activity such as mining sessions, daily streaks, reward claims, task claims, referral activity, wallet reward balances, transaction history, and app settings.
  • Wallet-related app data that you choose to create or import in the app, including locally stored wallet setup status. Never share your recovery phrase with anyone.
  • Payment/subscription status when Pro or boost features are enabled, including RevenueCat customer identifiers, product identifiers, entitlement status, and expiry dates.
  • Advertising and analytics data such as ad impressions, rewarded ad events, campaign performance, crash diagnostics, and device/app performance data.
  • Support communications you send to us, including deletion requests and security reports.

How we use data

  • To create and manage your Blockrium account.
  • To provide rewards, mining-session tracking, daily streaks, task rewards, referrals, wallet balance views, notifications, and app update controls.
  • To detect abuse, protect accounts, investigate security events, and prevent fraud.
  • To provide ads and rewarded ad placements, including optional rewards when an eligible ad is completed.
  • To manage subscriptions, Pro status, and boost eligibility through RevenueCat and backend systems.
  • To respond to support, deletion, legal, and compliance requests.
  • To improve app reliability, performance, navigation, and user experience.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers needed to operate the app:

  • Hosting, backend, database, security, notification, and email providers.
  • Advertising and mediation partners such as Appodeal and Google AdMob for ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and rewarded ad delivery.
  • Subscription management providers such as RevenueCat when subscription or boost features are used.
  • Analytics, crash reporting, and performance tools used to keep the app stable.
  • Law enforcement, regulators, or legal parties when required by applicable law or to protect our rights and users.

Advertising and rewarded ads

Blockrium may show rewarded ads, interstitial ads, banners, or sponsor placements. Ad partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, impression/click data, and similar signals to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Reward claims may continue without an ad when no ad inventory is available.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we may send mining-session reminders, daily reward availability, referral updates, wallet activity notices, launchpad announcements, security alerts, and admin campaign messages. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Wallet and blockchain data

Blockrium wallet features may let you create or import wallet information on your device. Blockchain transactions are public and cannot be deleted by Blockrium. Keep your recovery phrase private. Blockrium will never ask you to share your recovery phrase through support, email, or social media.

Data retention

  • We retain account data while your account is active.
  • Reward, wallet balance, referral, security, and transaction records may be retained as needed for fraud prevention, dispute handling, tax, legal, and regulatory reasons.
  • Server backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days before normal backup rotation removes it.
  • Public blockchain records cannot be removed from blockchain networks.

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Blockrium account and associated app data at https://blockrium.com/account-deletion/ or by emailing support@blockrium.com from your registered email address. Verified deletion requests are processed within 3 working days.

Your choices

  • You can update profile details in the app where available.
  • You can disable notifications in device settings.
  • You can reset or limit advertising identifiers using device settings where supported.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting support.

Children

Blockrium is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect user data. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please protect your password, device, wallet keys, and recovery phrase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, partners, laws, or security practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with the effective date.

Contact

For privacy, security, or account deletion questions, contact Blockrium Web3 Technologies at support@blockrium.com.
